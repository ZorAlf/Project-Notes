Chapter 17 — Break up with Your Phones

Why We’re Addicted to Phones? Phones are designed to capture and hold our attention using features like notifications, endless scrolling, and gamified elements. Dopamine plays a significant role in keeping us hooked by rewarding our brain for device interactions. The Impact of Phone Overuse Reduced focus and creativity. Increased anxiety and feelings of inadequacy, often due to social media. Disruption of real-life connections and experiences. Steps to Break the Addiction Awareness: Understand your habits by tracking screen time and identifying triggers. Boundaries: Set rules for phone use, such as no-phone zones or times. Mindfulness: Replace impulsive scrolling with purposeful actions, like reading or hobbies. Building a Healthier Relationship Use your phone as a tool, not a constant companion. Turn off unnecessary notifications. Prioritize face-to-face interactions and offline activities.
